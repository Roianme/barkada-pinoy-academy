About Barkada Pinoy Academy

Founded with the mission to preserve and share Filipino culture, we are dedicated to creating meaningful connections through language learning and cultural education.

Our Story

Building Bridges Through Culture

Barkada Pinoy Academy was established in 2018 by a group of Filipino educators and community leaders who recognized the need for authentic cultural education programs. The word "Barkada" means "group of friends" in Filipino, reflecting our core belief that learning happens best in a supportive, friendly environment.

What started as small community classes has grown into a comprehensive academy offering various programs for learners of all ages and backgrounds. We serve both Filipino diaspora communities wanting to reconnect with their heritage and international students eager to explore Filipino culture.

Today, we are proud to have helped thousands of students develop their language skills, deepen their cultural understanding, and build lasting friendships within our community.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality Filipino language and cultural education that fosters understanding, appreciation, and connection to Filipino heritage while building bridges between communities.

Our Vision

To be the leading institution for Filipino cultural education, creating a global network of learners who celebrate and preserve Filipino heritage for future generations.

Our Values

Community

We believe in the power of community and creating supportive learning environments where everyone feels welcome.

Authenticity

We are committed to providing authentic cultural experiences and accurate language instruction.

Inclusivity

We welcome learners of all backgrounds and abilities, celebrating diversity within our community.

Passion

Our work is driven by a genuine love for Filipino culture and a desire to share it with the world.
